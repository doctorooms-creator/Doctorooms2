## 0. DIAGNOSIS — Why The Current Structure Fails To Convert

| # | Current flaw | Why it kills conversion |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Feature-table thinking** ("IPD ❌ / ✅") | Doctors don't buy features. They buy outcomes: more income, less headache, professional status. |
| 2 | **No ROI math anywhere** | A doctor sees ₹9,999 and thinks "kharcha." Nobody showed him it returns ₹40,000+/mo. Price without value framing = expense. |
| 3 | **No risk reversal** | "What if I pay and my staff can't use it?" → paralysis. No guarantee = no first purchase. |
| 4 | **No urgency or scarcity** | "I'll do it later" = never. Zero reasons to act NOW. |
| 5 | **No emotional hook** | Where's the pain? The phone buzzing with "doctor sahab, number kab aayega?" The empty 11:30 slot that was a no-show. |
| 6 | **Free tier sells FEATURES, paid tier sells MORE features** | Wrong axis. The correct axis: **Free runs the clinic. Paid GROWS the practice.** |
| 7 | **No in-product conversion moments** | Pricing page converts 1-2% of visitors. The PRODUCT ITSELF converts 5-10x better at emotional moments. |

**The reframe (our master positioning line):**

> ### "Doctorooms Free aapka OPD chalata hai. Doctorooms Pro aapka practice badhata hai."
> Free = fully-functional clinic → maximum registrations (user's goal).
> Pro = growth + money + professionalism → conversion (revenue goal).

## 1. THE CUSTOMER (ICP Psychology — Solo Clinic Doctor, India)

### 1.1 His daily reality
- 20–30 patients/day OPD, ₹400–800 consultation fee
- Phone constantly interrupting: patients asking queue status, receptionist asking questions
- Hand-written Rx: 30–45 sec each, 2–3 hrs/day on paperwork
- **No-shows: industry benchmark 15–30% of booked slots** (Indian OPD reality)
- Zero recall system → patients drift to other doctors, lifetime value lost
- Cash-based practice; money sits in current account; HATES small recurring deductions
- Deep aspiration: "One day I'll open my own hospital / add a second doctor"

### 1.2 His decision triggers (what actually opens the wallet)
1. **Visible money loss** (the no-show who took a ₹600 slot and vanished)
2. **Time agony** (today's packed OPD, Rx writing eating his evening)
3. **Status anxiety** (the multi-specialty hospital next door looks "professional," he looks small)
4. **Growth ambition** (2nd doctor, own nursing home)
5. **A doctor-friend's recommendation** (peer trust > all advertising)

### 1.3 His objections (must be pre-handled)
| Objection | Answer (build into pricing page FAQ) |
|---|---|
| "Mera data kahan jayega? Kya loss ho jayega?" | Data India mein, export anytime CSV/PDF, cancel = data 90 din tak safe download ke liye |
| "Meri staff computer nahi chala payegi" | Receptionist 10-minute training video; WhatsApp pe bookings staff phone se bhi |
| "Main already Practo/HealthPlix use karta hoon" | Side-by-side comparison table (below); data import help free |
| "Paisa waste hoga to?" | 60-day ROI guarantee (below) |
| "Internet jata hai to clinic ruk jayegi" | Queue + Rx offline-first mode (roadmap); print never blocks |

## 2. THE OFFER ARCHITECTURE (Value Equation)

**Value = (Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood) ÷ (Time-to-Value × Effort)**

For each plan, maximize numerator, crush denominator:

| Element | How we maximize it |
|---|---|
| Dream outcome | Quantified income: recovered no-shows, extra daily slots, staff time saved |
| Perceived likelihood | 60-day guarantee + case studies + live usage ticker ("1,214 doctors used this today") |
| Time-to-value | Onboarding promise: **"10 minute mein pehla patient book"** — import 10 patients, live |
| Effort & sacrifice | ₹0 to start; WhatsApp-based staff flow; no hardware; no training packages |

### 2.1 The Pro Value Stack (what the pricing page SHOWS)

**"Doctorooms Clinic Pro — ₹833/month (₹9,999/year)"**

What the doctor GETS (monthly value framing):
| Included | Worth/month |
|---|---|
| WhatsApp auto-reminders (no-show recovery: ~6 slots × ₹600) | ₹3,600 |
| Patient recall campaigns (dormant patients wapas) | ₹2,400 |
| AI Copilot: Rx + summaries (2 hrs/day time saved) | ₹3,000 |
| Money dashboard + no-show recovery report | "priceless" → ₹500 |
| Staff accounts (3 receptionist + 3 nurse seats) | ₹1,200 |
| **Total value** | **₹10,700/month** |
| **Aapki price** | **₹833/month** |

> Headline: **"Ek patient ki fees = poore mahine ka software."**
> (One consultation fee @₹600-800 covers the month. This single line does more than the whole feature table — it reframes ₹9,999 from "expense" to "rounding error.")

### 2.2 Hospital Pro Value Stack (for 20+ bed facilities)

- Hero claim: **"IPD billing leakage 5–15% hota hai — hum rok dete hain."**
  - 30-bed hospital, ₹40L/mo billing, even 3% leakage recovery = ₹1.2L/mo recovered vs ₹5,000/mo plan price → **24x ROI story**
- Includes: charge master, insurance pre-auth tracking, IPD billing, OT scheduling, department analytics, 10 doctor seats
- Anchor line: "Ek leakage-recovered bill, poora saal ka software."

## 3. THE REVISED PLAN MATRIX (Persuasion-Optimized)

### 3.1 Core changes vs v1
1. **Names = identity, not tiers:** Starter → **"Free"** (zero risk word), Clinic Pro → **"Pro"** (identity: professional), Hospital stays **"Hospital Pro"** (aspirational for nursing-home doctors).
2. **Hero features of paid = MONEY + GROWTH features**, not ops features: WhatsApp automation, recall campaigns, no-show recovery report, money dashboard, online presence page (new patient magnet).
3. **Free tier gets a taste of every habit-forming paid feature** (20 WhatsApp reminders/mo, 50 AI credits) — habit forms → wall arrives → upgrade is a relief, not a pitch.
4. Analytics split: Free sees today; Pro sees the **"Lost Revenue" report** (this report is THE conversion weapon — it makes the pain numerical).

## 4. PRICE PRESENTATION & ANCHORING (Pricing Page Blueprint)

**Page order (top → bottom):**

1. **ROI calculator first** (interactive, before any plan card):
   > "Aapki fees ₹___ per visit · ___ no-shows/month · isse hum bacha sakte hain ₹___/mo"
   > Output: "Aapka monthly loss: ₹18,400. Doctorooms Pro: ₹833. **Net profit from month 1: ₹17,567.**"
2. **Three plan cards** — middle card (Pro) visually dominant: bigger, badge "**SABSE POPULAR — 87% doctors yahi lete hain**", price crossed ₹1,199 → ₹833/mo effect
3. **Value stack** under Pro (the ₹10,700 vs ₹833 table)
4. **Comparison table vs HealthPlix & Practo Ray** (price + staff accounts + hospital path + AI + WhatsApp — honest, verifiable): "Same budget. Zyada software. Hospital tak ka raasta."
5. **Trust strip:** "Data India mein · DPDP compliant · ABDM-ready · 60-day guarantee"
6. **FAQ objection handlers** (from §1.3)
7. **Founder offer banner** (urgency — §5)

**Anchoring rules:**
- Never say "₹9,999/year" alone — always pair: "₹833/month · annual billing (2 months free)"
- Always sit price NEXT TO recovered-revenue number (₹833 next to ₹3,600 saved)
- Hospital Pro anchors Pro (₹59,999 makes ₹9,999 look small — classic decoy; a ₹59,999 sighted once makes the Pro decision feel safe)

## 6. IN-PRODUCT CONVERSION ENGINE (The Real Revenue Machine)

A pricing page converts ~1–2%. **The product converts 5–10x** if upgrade moments are engineered:

| Moment (emotional peak) | Trigger | Message |
|---|---|---|
| Week 2, Friday evening | 8+ no-shows logged | Dashboard banner: "Is hafte 9 no-shows = ₹5,400 gaya. Pro ke auto-reminders se 80% bach sakte the." + [1-click upgrade] |
| Rx #50 printed | Counter | "Aapne 50 prescriptions likhi Doctorooms se — 3 ghante bachaye. Badhai! 🎉" + upgrade nudge |
| Patient #100 in records | Counter | "Aapki puri practice ab digital hai. Ise protect karo + analytics unlock karo → Pro" |
| Receptionist adds 2nd staff | Seat wall | Growth-celebration framing (never "denied") |
| Doctor checks earnings page | Weekly | "February mein aapne ₹1.4L kamaye. No-shows ne ₹21,000 kata. Yeh report Pro mein full aati hai." |
| End of trial day 12 | Trial clock | WhatsApp + in-app: "2 din bache. Aapke 14 din ke data: 61 patients, 58 Rx, 4.5 hrs bachay. Pro mein sab + unlimited." |

**Trial summary email/WhatsApp (day 14) = personalized usage receipt** — the strongest conversion artifact we'll have. Numbers about THEIR OWN practice.
