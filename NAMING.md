## 🧪 Har naam pe ye 5 filter lagao (final list banane ke liye)
1. **Taxi-driver test** — auto wale ko ek baar sunao, doosri baar wo khud bol sake? (BharatPe pass karta hai, "Aestra" fail karta hai — ye tradeoff samajh ke chalo)
2. **WhatsApp test** — chat me type karna easy ho, typo na ho, "kaun sa spelling?" confusion na ho
3. **Store test** — 12-13 characters ke andar, icon ke sath clear dikhe, App Store/Play pe search ho jaye
4. **Story test** — 1 line me legend/pitch bol sako? ("Naam me chhupi kahani" — Claude/Aestra style)
5. **Domain + TM test** — .com/.in/.health + Trademark Class 5 (pharma) / 9 (app) / 42 (SaaS)

## 📊 Domain khud kaise check karo (10-second method, tool ki zaroorat nahi)
Browser me kholo:
```
https://dns.google/resolve?name=NAAM.com&type=NS
```
- `"Status": 3` → **AVAILABLE** (register hi nahi hua)
- `"Status": 0` → TAKEN

`.in`, `.health`, `.care`, `.app` — sab same method se. **Ye first-pass hai** — final purchase se pehle GoDaddy/Cloudflare pe confirm karo (kabhi-kabhi NXDOMAIN bhi hota hai freshly expired domains pe).

## 🧭 Strategy note (is file ka sabse important 3 line)
Tumhara product **two-sided** hai: doctor ka HMS + patient ka app.
- **Relationship words** (Sakha, Bandhu, Mitra, Humsafar, Apna) → **dono side** pe kaam karte hain
- **Treatment words** (Ilaaj, Shifa, Upchar, Nidaan) → patient-side strong, doctor-side thoda weak
- **Institutional words** (Sehat, Arogya, Jan-) → platform-grade, neutral, bharosa wala

Jo side tumhara PRIMARY brand hai (doctor-first ya patient-first), us hisab se axis chuno.
